Monday

You have just been promoted to team lead. It is 9:00 on a Monday, and the messages are already arriving.

  • Open a message, then choose what to do with it: do it now, delegate, schedule, or decline.
  • Time passes only when you act. Everything you do uses minutes and focus.
  • Your team impact rises and falls with your choices. Some consequences arrive hours later.
  • At 5:00 pm you see how you did, what pattern showed up, and how you compare with your last attempt.

Keyboard: arrow keys move through the inbox, and keys 1 to 4 choose an action. About 10 to 15 minutes. Nothing is graded.
