About Normand

I'm Normand Theriault, an instructional designer and college instructor based near Houston, Texas. I hold a PhD in E-Learning, master's degrees in philosophy and English literature, and a bachelor's in creative writing.

I have taught college courses since 2008, and today I teach philosophy, ethics, and humanities fully online. That mix shapes the work: I write like a teacher, structure like a designer, and build the result myself.

The name comes from limen, the Latin word for threshold. It is the point where a learner has left what they knew and hasn't yet arrived at what they can do. Good design is what makes that crossing short.
